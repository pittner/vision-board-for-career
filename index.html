12 year-end questions before you choose

Answer them alone, with a notebook, in 15 minutes. Be honest, nobody else reads it. The answers that surprise you point to your career goals.

  1. What work in 2026 made me proud?
  2. What did I do that gave me energy, and what drained it?
  3. Which result of mine helped the company the most?
  4. What did I learn this year that I did not know a year ago?
  5. Am I paid fairly for what I do now?
  6. Who helped my career this year, and did I thank them?
  7. What do I want to stop doing at work in 2027?
  8. If I stay in this job, where am I in two years?
  9. If I leave, what would I miss and what would I not?
  10. Which skill would most raise my value next year?
  11. What would my ideal workday look like?
  12. If 2027 goes really well at work, what is different a year from now?

Plan your work year in 45 minutes

  1. 10 minutes: look back. Write three work wins and three frustrations of 2026. Open your calendar and sent emails to remember what you really did.
  2. 15 minutes: answer the questions. Go through the 12 questions below. Short, honest answers are enough.
  3. 10 minutes: pick three career goals. One for pay or the next role, one for skills, one for your balance or your own project.
  4. 10 minutes: make it visible. Turn your most important goal and its first step into a phone lock screen, print it for your desk, or make a full board.

Questions

What is a career vision board?

A board with your work goals for the coming year, such as a raise, a promotion, a new job, a new skill or more balance. It keeps your career goals in front of you, so busy weeks do not push them aside.

How do you make a career vision board?

Look back at your work year, answer a few honest questions, pick three career goals and make them visible. This page has 36 career goals with a first step, 12 year-end career questions and a 45-minute plan.

What should I put on a career vision board?

Goals for pay and promotion, a new job or field, skills, your network, work-life balance and your own projects. Add one first step to each goal so the board says what to do this week, not only what to hope for.

Does a vision board help with getting a new job or a raise?

It helps if it is concrete. A number, a date and a first step turn a wish into a plan you see every time you unlock your phone.
