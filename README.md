# Career Vision Board 2027

36 career goals in 6 groups (pay and promotion, new job or career change, skills and learning, visibility and network, work-life balance, own projects and money). Each goal has why it matters and a first step for this week, plus 12 honest year-end career questions and a 45-minute plan.

**Live page:** https://pittner.github.io/vision-board-for-career/

- Press a goal and it opens in the [free vision board maker](https://visionboard.bemooore.com/free-vision-board-maker/?mode=wallpaper&layout=text&utm_source=github_readme&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=career_2027) with the first step under it; save it as a 1080x1920 lock screen or print it for your desk
- Or type your own career goal and first step
- No signup, nothing uploaded, MIT licensed

Rebuild: `python3 build.py` (no dependencies)
